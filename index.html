Put a team on the board

Anyone can enter, right up to the gun and during the race. One entry per team — then keep this page open on one phone and tap it each time you come through the arch.

Both formats cover the same 50 km. Pairs split the loops however they like — alternate, blocks, whatever survives.

How the dash works

Fifty laps of the same kilometre. Start together at 10:00, finish whenever your fiftieth loop lands. Donuts sit at the arch. Drinks you bring yourself.
